I am just back from vacation for a week, so sorry for the delay. You missed
one field - timecode. The record is all  ASCII and as I recall the field
units are as follows:

 Year - four digit integer

 Day - three digit integer  Julian day, in the common usage

Hour - decimal universal coordinated time accurate to 0.004 (the Point
Lobos standard clock)

Timecode - hour:minutes:seconds:frame, where each  portion is a two digit
integer, (with leading zero if necessary) and there are 30 frames to the
second.

Frame -  this is timecode converted to an absolute integer sequential frame
number..using 30 frames a second, 1800 frames a minute, etc. the idea being
that timecode and frame number are unique addresses to a specific frame on
each tape. In standard usage, that is, tape counter zeroed at the beginning
of a dive and not reset as tapes are added, this number is the unique and
absolute address of each frame on a dive.....so dive number and this number
identify the frame.

Depth - ROV ParoScientific depth...recently discontinued...

Focus- 10 bit signed integer representing raw telemetry from the focus
feedback pot.

Zoom - 10 bit signed integer representing raw telemetry from the zoom
feedback pot.

Iris- 10 bit signed integer representing raw telemetry from the iris
feedback pot.

Fieldwidth- width of the image in centimeters , calculated from the
calibrated focus and zoom telemetry data above using a two variable
approximation as explained in the Camera Control User's Guide Appendix and
the paper, Daniel Davis,  "Measurements with Underwater Video: Camera
FieldWidth Calibration and Structured Lighting" , Journal of the Marine
Technology Society, Vol 26., No. 4, Winter, 1992-3.

Hope this helps...
Dan
