Precruise and postcruise report notification

When a cruise report is created or edited, the information is not immediately added to the database (unless the edits are made by a registered "power user"). The report is submitted by e-mail to the Logistics Coordinator, who reviews it and loads it into the database if it passes inspection. When reports are loaded into the database, an e-mail is generated to the precruise' and 'postcruise' e-mail aliases with all the information in the report.

Below are examples of the e-mails sent to 'precruise' and 'postcruise' e-mail aliases when the cruise reports are loaded into the database. The format is similar to the e-mail message originally received by the Logistics Coordinator when the user submitted the edits. At the bottom of the page is a link to re-edit the cruise report.
